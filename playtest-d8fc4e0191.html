Playtest

Thanks for helping. This is an early prototype: things will break, look rough or feel slow, and that's exactly what we want to hear about. The question we're trying to answer is simple: is gathering, crafting and building together fun?

Getting in

  1. Download the game for Windows and unzip it somewhere you can write to, like Downloads or Documents (not Program Files).

    Download for Windows About 100 MB. Always the newest version.

  2. Run GhostInTheGrove.exe. Windows may say it's from an unknown publisher, because the game isn't signed yet: click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Join the playtest server (play.ghostinthegrove.com): it's already in the list on the start screen. Pick it and click Join.
  4. Make an account the first time: any name and a password. Tick Remember me and next time Join logs you straight in. Your character and everything you build stay on the server.
  5. Updates install themselves. When a new version is out, the start screen offers Update and restart.

Things to try

Roughly in this order, but go wherever curiosity takes you.

  1. Build a first shelter with bare hands

    Pull sticks from an oak with E, pick plant fibre from a bush, twist it into cord (C), and put up a stick floor, walls and a thatch roof.

  2. Make flint tools

    Find flint on the beaches and rocky ground, knap blades, and make a knife, an axe and a pickaxe. Notice how a tool changes what you gather and how fast.

  3. Set up a workbench

    Place it anywhere on the ground or a floor. It turns oak into planks and stone into blocks.

  4. Claim your land

    A claim post keeps the land within 25 metres yours: nobody else can build, dig, gather or open your chests there. Hold B to find it on the build wheel.

  5. Store your things

    Build a storage chest and press E beside it to move stacks between it and your pack.

  6. Build a smithy

    A forge needs a stone floor under it. Add an anvil to work iron, bellows for better ingots and a smithing bench for better tools, then make iron tools.

  7. Chase good materials

    Every resource has quality stats, and better inputs make better tools and stronger buildings. Some parts of the island have better iron, stone or oak than others.

  8. Build something you're proud of

    A two-storey house, a roofed workshop, a tower: whatever you like. Flatten or raise the ground with T to make room. Build near the others and say hello in chat.

Telling us what you think

  • Saw something odd, broken or fun? Press F2. The game takes a screenshot of exactly what you see, then asks what was going on. The host gets your note and the screenshot, with where you were standing, your frame rate and ping, and your game version. Untick the screenshot if you'd rather not send it.
  • After a session, send the host a few lines: how much fun it was, how far you got, the best moment, what was frustrating or confusing, and the one thing you'd change.
  • For a bug report, say what you did, what happened and what you expected. Press F2 as soon as it happens, so the screenshot shows it.
  • If the game crashes or freezes, its log is in %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Ghost in the Grove\logs.
